"The car has become the carapace, the protective and aggressive shell, of urban and suburban man." — Marshall McLuhan

Apex Car Club is a community for those who see driving as more than transportation. Founded on the belief that the right road, in the right machine, is one of life's genuine pleasures.

We welcome all makes, all eras, and all levels of experience. What matters is the passion. Our events range from relaxed Sunday morning drives to competitive track days at some of the UK's finest circuits.
